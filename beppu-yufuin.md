# Beppu / Yufuin

**Days:** 2 nights (together with friend), onsen ryokan stay

## Overview

The genuine slow-down stretch of the trip — 2 nights at an onsen ryokan rather than
splitting a night with Fukuoka. Wants a hike/scenic viewpoint worked in (not purely
low-key), and lodging needs to be tattoo-friendly with a private bath option (see
trip-wide notes).

## Recommendations by theme

### Nature / hike (the anchor for the "want a hike" ask)
- **Mount Tsurumi + Beppu Ropeway** — 1,375m peak overlooking Beppu, panoramic
  views to Mount Yufu and the Kuju Mountains on a clear day. Ropeway up takes
  ~10 min (¥1,600-1,800 round trip) with a 15-min walk to the actual summit; full
  hike up takes ~2-3 hours for a more demanding option. Given the ask for a real
  hike (not just a viewpoint), doing the hike one way and the ropeway the other is
  a good balance.
- **February timing bonus**: winter (Dec-mid March) brings hoarfrost coating the
  trees near the summit in a crystalline ice layer — a seasonal effect specific to
  this time of year, not a lesser-season compromise.
- Small shrine at the summit (Tsurumi Sanjo Gongen) — a touch of traditional/
  historic along the way.
- **Wind-closure risk**: February is a windier month and the ropeway closes
  irregularly in high wind — worth having a backup day/plan in mind. Backups that
  don't depend on the ropeway at all:
  - **Mount Tsurumi via the hiking trail** — same mountain, same views, entirely
    on foot (base to summit and back, ~2-3 hrs each way), unaffected by ropeway
    wind closures.
  - **Mount Yufu (Yufu-dake)** — standalone hike near Yufuin, recognizable
    twin-peak profile, no cable car involved at all so wind doesn't affect it.
    More physically demanding than Tsurumi (real elevation gain, exposed sections
    near the peaks) — worth checking the group's hiking fitness before committing.
  - **Kannawa hot spring district walk** — lower-key fallback if weather turns bad
    enough that neither mountain hike feels appealing.

### Sightseeing (low priority — treat as filler, not planned time)
- **Jigoku Meguri (Hells Tour)** — viewing-only tour of colorful, boiling mineral
  hot spring ponds around Beppu (not for bathing). Rated "maybe, low priority" —
  slot as an if-time option on the lighter of the two days rather than planning
  around it.

### Food
- **Yudofu** (hot pot tofu) and other tofu-based dishes are a specialty of onsen
  towns like this — good vegetarian option, unlike some of the meat-heavy
  specialties elsewhere on the trip.
- If a ryokan kaiseki dinner is included with lodging, flag the vegetarian
  requirement when booking — many can accommodate with advance notice.

### Lodging (carried over from earlier trip-wide notes)
- Needs tattoo-friendly, private-bath (kashikiri/kazoku-buro) ryokan — see
  README.md trip constants for the reasoning.
