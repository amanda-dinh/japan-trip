# Fukuoka

**Days:** ~half day (transit stop en route from Beppu/Yufuin to Tokyo, together with friend)

## Overview

Food-focused stop, since it's short. Willing to flex vegetarian for the real Hakata
ramen experience rather than hunting for a veg-only version.

## Recommendations by theme

### Food (main focus)
- **Hakata ramen** — the city's signature dish: rich, milky tonkotsu (pork bone)
  broth, thin firm noodles. Well-regarded spots: **Ichiran** (famous individual-booth
  chain, originated here) and **Shin Shin**. Most counters open 11am-midnight, some
  24-hour spots near Nakasu — fits naturally into an evening.
  - If ever wanting a vegetarian version without flexing: **BUGORO All Vegan** makes
    a vegan Hakata-style ramen.
- **Yatai (open-air stalls)** — Nakasu and Tenjin are the main districts, tiny
  stalls along the river serving ramen, yakitori, oden, etc. Don't operate in heavy
  rain — check the evening forecast if planning a specific night around this.
- **Mentaiko** (spicy marinated cod roe) — beloved local specialty; often not
  vegetarian-compatible as a dish itself, but a good food-culture souvenir
  (vacuum-packed, keeps 2-3 weeks refrigerated).

### Nature (group loves this category)
- **Ohori Park** — large park built around a lake (former outer moat of Fukuoka
  Castle), walking paths circling the water; genuine green space in the city,
  easy half-day fit.
- **Uminonakamichi Seaside Park** — coastal park further out, beaches, flower
  fields, more expansive than Ohori if there's time for it.
