# Kyoto

**Days:** ~3 (solo, while friend is in Hokkaido)

## Overview

Kyoto has enough beyond temples that visiting a handful of the standout ones (rather
than capping at 2) makes sense — the top picks are each visually distinct rather than
repetitive, so it doesn't feel like a checklist.

## Recommendations by theme

### Traditional / historic (temples & shrines)
- **Fushimi Inari Taisha** — thousands of vermilion torii gates up the mountainside,
  free, no closing time (great early morning before crowds). Absolute must — unlike
  anything else on the trip.
- **Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)** — gold-leaf building over a reflecting pond, very
  photogenic, quick visit (~1 hour).
- **Kiyomizu-dera** — wooden temple on a hillside with a large viewing stage, near the
  Higashiyama historic streets (Sannenzaka/Ninenzaka) — pairs naturally with
  wandering/shopping rather than being a standalone stop.
- Optional 4th: **Ginkaku-ji (Silver Pavilion)** + the **Philosopher's Path** —
  quieter, less crowded than the others.

### Hands-on traditional experience
- **Kimono rental + walking the historic streets** (Gion/Higashiyama) is probably the
  most efficient combo of the options — get the experience and a scenic backdrop in
  one go, pairs naturally with the Kiyomizu-dera walk.
- Tea ceremony also widely available, many English-friendly options in the
  Gion/central area (~1 hour), if wanted in addition.

### Food
- **Nishiki Market** — covered shopping street, lots of food stalls for grazing;
  scout vegetarian options ahead, some stalls are seafood/skewer-heavy.
- **Pontocho Alley** — atmospheric riverside dining alley, more sit-down.

### Nature / scenery (group loves this category)
- **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove** — nice-to-have, not a must (per stated priority); slot
  as a half-day add-on paired with nearby Tenryu-ji temple grounds, not a full day.
- **Fushimi Inari's mountain trail** — the torii-gate path continues up Mt. Inari
  itself; most visitors turn back after the first viewpoint, but the fuller hike
  moves into genuine forest and is much quieter than the crowded lower gates.
- **Philosopher's Path** — canal-side walking path near Ginkaku-ji, lined with
  cherry trees (not blooming in February, but still a quiet forested walk).

### Neighborhood wandering
- **Gion** — historic geisha district, good for an evening walk (possible
  geisha/maiko sightings near dusk around Hanamikoji-dori).
