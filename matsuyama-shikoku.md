# Matsuyama (Shikoku)

**Days:** 1 (transit day, ferry-to-ferry, together with friend)

## Overview

A light, food/wandering-focused stopover between the Hiroshima and Beppu ferry legs,
rather than a packed sightseeing day. Skipping Dogo Onsen bathing (already getting
onsen time in Beppu/Yufuin) and treating the castle as "only if convenient."

## Ferry logistics (important for the day's timing)

- **Hiroshima → Matsuyama**: high-speed jet, ~70 minutes, roughly 8 daily
  departures. Faster than the car ferry option (2.5 hrs) — book the jet.
- **Matsuyama → Beppu**: less direct. Matsuyama connects to Yawatahama port
  (~1 hour by bus/train), then a separate ferry from Yawatahama to Beppu takes
  about 2 hours 50 minutes. Worth checking for an overnight sailing option on this
  leg specifically — some regional routes run overnight, which could mean sleeping
  through the crossing instead of losing daytime hours. Confirm once closer to
  booking.

## Recommendations by theme

### If convenient
- **Matsuyama Castle** — central, reachable by tram, ropeway/chairlift up to the
  keep.

### Architecture (quick look, not a bath)
- **Dogo Onsen Honkan building** — striking historic bathhouse architecture, often
  cited as inspiration for the bathhouse in *Spirited Away*; worth a look from
  outside even while skipping the actual bath.

### Food / wandering
- **Okaido / Gintengai shopping arcades** — covered shopping streets in central
  Matsuyama, good for casual food and browsing between ferries.
- **Taruto** — local specialty, rolled sponge cake with sweet bean paste, good food
  souvenir.

### Nature (group loves this category)
- **Shiroyama Park** — the forested hill the castle sits on; the ropeway/chairlift
  ride up passes through wooded parkland, so even a "castle if convenient" visit
  doubles as a green-space walk.
- Matsuyama is a gateway to **Mount Ishizuchi**, the highest peak in Shikoku, but
  it's a longer trip out of the city — likely not realistic given this is a
  ferry-to-ferry transit day; flagging for a future, longer Shikoku visit instead.
