# Osaka

**Days:** 1 (day trip/stop, solo, while friend is in Hokkaido)

## Overview

Osaka is nicknamed "Japan's Kitchen" — food is the main draw here. Skipping
Universal Studios Japan (needs a full day to be worth it) and treating Osaka Castle
as skippable (not naturally on the way) frees the single day for food and
neighborhood wandering.

## Recommendations by theme

### Food (main focus)
- **Dotonbori** — iconic neon-lit canal strip, great for evening atmosphere and
  grazing. Takoyaki (octopus balls) is the famous street food here but isn't
  vegetarian; look for **negiyaki** or veggie-adapted **okonomiyaki** instead — many
  stalls can do a vegetable-only version on request, since the batter itself is
  often vegetarian-friendly aside from the toppings.
- **Kuromon Ichiba Market** — covered market (Osaka's answer to Kyoto's Nishiki),
  good for grazing a variety of stalls in one place.
- **Shinsekai** — older, retro district known for kushikatsu (fried skewers) —
  traditionally mostly meat/seafood, but the format adapts well to vegetables if
  seeking out veg-specific stalls.

### Nightlife
- Dotonbori doubles as the nightlife area — canal + neon signs are as much the
  destination at night as the food, covers this interest without a separate stop.

### Architecture / design (if time allows)
- **Umeda Sky Building** — modern architecture, rooftop observation deck connecting
  two towers via a floating garden walkway. Good for a design/skyline moment without
  much time commitment.

### Nature (group loves this category — honest note: Osaka is thin here)
- **Osaka Castle Park** — if the castle itself ends up being "on the way," the
  surrounding park has a moat and plum/cherry tree groves (not blooming in
  February, but still a green space to walk).
- **Nakanoshima Park** — riverside park between two branches of the Yodo River,
  central and walkable, more of a quiet stroll than a destination in itself.
- Realistically, Osaka's a food/urban day more than a nature day — if the group
  wants a real nature fix, that's better spent in Kyoto/Nara (already well covered)
  or the Kyushu/onsen leg rather than trying to manufacture it here.

### Skipped
- **Universal Studios Japan** — needs a full day to be worth it, not happening with
  only 1 day here.
- **Osaka Castle** — only worth it if convenient/on the way; not built into the plan.
