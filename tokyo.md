# Tokyo

**Days:** 3 (Day 1 arrival/jet lag, Day 2 full day with friend, Day 3 extra day before she flies out)

## Overview

First stop, together with friend for all 3 days. Friend's only specific ask here is
one full day covering the icons + a Pokemon Center. Everything else is open.

## Recommendations by theme

### Food
- Vegetarian-friendly is genuinely strong in Tokyo now, but traditional dishes often
  use dashi (fish stock) — worth targeting dishes/restaurants built to be vegetarian
  rather than assuming.
- **Shojin ryori** — Buddhist temple cuisine, fully vegetarian, historically rooted;
  fits the traditional-sites interest too.
- **Depachika** (department store basement food halls) — good for a mixed-diet group,
  everyone picks their own thing.
- **Izakaya-style** — small shareable plates work well for a group with different
  diets, no one is stuck ordering a single fixed dish.

### Pop culture / anime / gaming
- **Akihabara** — arcades, retro game shops, anime merch, maid cafes.
- **Nakano Broadway** — quieter, more collector-focused alternative to Akihabara.
- **Pokemon Center** — confirm which location (Shibuya vs. Ikebukuro Sunshine City
  vs. others) before the day it's slotted into.

### Art & design
- **teamLab** (Planets and/or Borderless exhibits) — immersive digital art,
  photogenic, fits "iconic in small doses."
- **Daikanyama / Nakameguro** — slower, boutique/gallery neighborhoods, less
  crowded than Shibuya/Harajuku if a design-focused breather is wanted.

### Nightlife / bars
- **Golden Gai** (Shinjuku) — tiny themed bars in narrow alleys, iconic, good
  "small dose" of touristy nightlife.
- **Shimokitazawa** — more local/indie bar and vintage shop scene, less touristy
  contrast to Golden Gai.

### Traditional / historic
- **Asakusa (Senso-ji Temple, Nakamise-dori)** — most concentrated traditional
  atmosphere in central Tokyo, good low-key first day.
- **Meiji Shrine** — forested shrine grounds, next to Harajuku.
- **Imperial Palace East Gardens** — another traditional/historic option if more
  is wanted.

### Nature (group loves this category)
- **Meiji Shrine grounds** — genuinely forested (see above), a real green pocket
  in the middle of the city, not just a shrine courtyard.
- **Shinjuku Gyoen** — large formal garden park (English, French, and Japanese
  landscape sections), a step up in scale/quiet from Meiji Shrine if more nature
  time is wanted.
- **Yoyogi Park** — adjacent to Meiji Shrine, more open lawns and casual strolling
  space, easy to combine with a Harajuku/Shibuya day.

### Iconic / touristy (fine in small doses)
- **Shibuya Crossing**
- **Harajuku / Takeshita Street**
- **Shinjuku** (Omoide Yokocho for casual yakitori alley food)
